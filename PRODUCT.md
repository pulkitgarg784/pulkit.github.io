# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and engineers at graphics, engine, and games studios, evaluating Pulkit's technical depth. They usually arrive from a resume, LinkedIn, or a job application, scan for a few minutes, and decide whether to reach out or forward him to a hiring manager.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Pulkit Garg (pulkit.dev), Computer Science at the University of Waterloo (Computational Fine Arts, minor in Cognitive Science). Success: a graphics/engine/games visitor quickly believes he is a serious real-time graphics engineer, finds proof (projects, code, reel, resume), and contacts him.

## Positioning

A graphics programmer who ships rendering work in production (Vulkan viewport work on Houdini at SideFX) and also makes 3D art and games, so he understands both the engine and the people using it.

## Operating Context

Visitors read on desktop at work, often between other candidate tabs, and sometimes on a phone from a LinkedIn link. They open GitHub links, the resume PDF, and the reel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages (custom domain pulkit.dev). Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step.
- Must keep: hero intro, About copy, project list with category filters (Research, Tools, Games, Development), 3D portfolio YouTube reel, footer links, `/art` and `/resume` redirects, OG meta.
- The interactive hero piece (currently a cherry-blossom fractal tree) may be replaced.
- Existing colours (pink/peach) are not binding.

## Brand Commitments

None binding beyond his name and real content. The user explicitly rejects anything that reads as generic AI-generated design.

## Evidence on Hand

- Copy and links in `index.html` (SideFX, Liftwerx, Ford, Sojourn Interactive, NASA Space Apps result).
- `headshot.jpg`, `resume.pdf`, YouTube reel `3P80bAqu3zw`, ArtStation, itch.io, GitHub repos.
- No project screenshots in the repo; do not fabricate metrics or testimonials.

## Product Principles

- Proof over adjectives: every claim links to code, a build, or the reel.
- Readable in a two-minute scan by a busy recruiter.
- The site itself should demonstrate real-time graphics craft, without getting in the way of the content.
